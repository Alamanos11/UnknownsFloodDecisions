# Table 2 (draft) – the four decisions: who takes them in Greece, on what evidence today, and our metric
Checked 23/11/2026 on the consolidated law texts (taxheaven.gr) and official pages; to be re-read on et.gr before submission.

| Decision | Who decides (legal basis) | Evidence used today | Our metric (Section 3.4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** Which stream / reach gets a study (and a survey) first | Study programmes of Regions, Regional Units, municipalities and ministries; FRMP measures (FRMP_EL11_2025); delineation files can be prepared by the Ministry of Environment, the General Secretariat of Public Works, a Decentralised Administration, a Region, a municipality or private persons (N4258_2014 art. 2 §4). National information system for delineation in preparation (RRF_Delineation). | Past floods, complaints, political priority; FRMP APSFRs; no comparative hazard metric for small torrents | Rank stability of streams and of 71 reaches (buildings reached), Spearman ρ, P(top-10), 14 641 mixed-state combinations |
| **D2** Where maintenance (cleaning, desilting, unblocking) is worth the money | Maintenance works on streams: services of the ministries, the Decentralised Administration or the Region; delegable by decision of the Decentralised Administration's Secretary General (now Coordinator) to municipalities, Regional Units or Regions for streams within their area (N4258_2014 art. 7); in practice yearly cleaning contracts by Regional Units (E3) | Visual inspection, complaints, "cleaned and did not flood" arguments (E5); no modelled effect | Change in flooded area and buildings vs the same channel unmaintained (overgrown, cleared, half-blocked crossings, bed +0.5 m) |
| **D3** When to warn for a stream | Civil protection mechanism (N4662_2020): Risk Assessment Committee for weather phenomena (art. 7α, added by N5075_2023) sets alert levels per area; 112 messages to the public; Regional/local civil protection bodies act | National weather warnings and colour codes per regional unit (whole Π.Ε. Σερρών Red Code, 26/01/2026); no stream-specific thresholds | Outlet discharge (and, in P2, rainfall) at which 10/50/100 buildings are reached, per channel state |
| **D4** Where the legal line falls (plots/buildings in or out) | Delineation file checked (regional technical directorate where applicable) and lines validated by the Secretary General of the Decentralised Administration, the Minister of Environment or the Minister of Infrastructure, depending on who prepared the file; Presidential Decree in exceptional cases (N4258_2014 art. 3); file contents: topographic survey, hydrological and hydraulic study, flood and delineation lines (N4258_2014 art. 2; KYA2017) | Full study with survey (€0.1–0.75 M per stream); where none exists, case-by-case decisions | Line shift (median, P90) and the band where the line may fall over 14 plausible T50 states, with buildings inside |

Notes
- N4258_2014 art. 2 §5 provides for a "temporary delineation" based on a hydraulic study with specifications set by ministerial decision – relevant to whether a survey-free line could serve as interim (Discussion 5.1). To check whether such specifications were issued.
- Responsibility split Region – Forest Service for upland beds (E5) to mention in D2 discussion.
